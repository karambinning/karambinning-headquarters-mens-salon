The cut, unhurried.

Headquarters opened its doors on the 2nd of January, 2003. The room was professionally designed before the first appointment was taken, and twenty‑three years on it has been kept that way: clean, considered, and quietly proud of itself.

The work is straightforward — men's hair, cut by people who know how to cut it. The pace is the part most worth advertising. There is no clock on the wall asking you to hurry. There is, however, a leather sofa, fresh coffee, and Sky on the television if you would rather wait than rush.

The shelf is brief on purpose.

Two product houses, both of them good. American Crew for hold and finish. Wella SP for shampoos, conditioners and the things you want a barber's wife to use too.

No fifteen‑bottle wall. No private‑label gimmickry. The shelf is brief on purpose — what is stocked is stocked because we use it on the chair.
